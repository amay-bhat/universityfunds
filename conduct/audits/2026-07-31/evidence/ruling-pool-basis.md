# PHASE 2 — FINAL RULING: pool-basis percentages under a school's name

Senior (Fable, fresh context), two-phase blind protocol. Phase 1 preliminary: Option B with a ≥95% endowment-in-pool test, era-bracketing evidence clause, Stanford gap stands, FY2004 store-actual. Requester's Phase-1-blind recommendation: Option B with ≥90% endowment-share-OF-pool, per-year evidence kill condition, Stanford early years "possibly curatable."

## 1. Attack on the couldn't-verify list

### (a) FNL article authenticity — INTEGRATION MUST GATE ON ONE SUCCESSFUL INDEPENDENT FETCH.

The chain as it stands: one worker fetch + transcription, internally cross-validated (Figure 1 reconciles to Table II per category; all columns sum to 100.0 — I re-computed the three in-window columns myself), requester verified the transcription *exists in the fragment*, my own fetch attempt timed out, and the MIT QC's re-verification covered the congressional PDFs and Treasurer's Reports but **not** the FNL. So every path back to the primary document runs through a single session's uncorroborated fetch.

That is not sufficient under K1 for three curated years, for two reasons:

1. **The project's own QC standard.** The sampling plan re-fetches primary documents for spot-check years precisely because "numbers right, recorded reasoning wrong" and transcription errors are the observed failure class of this pilot. The FNL is not merely a spot-check candidate — it is the *sole* source for 3 of MIT's 7 allocation years, and the only source in the entire dataset that no second session has ever loaded. Internal consistency (sums to 100.0, pie-reconciles-to-table) proves faithful *transcription of something*; it cannot prove the something is the document at that URL. K1's line is "unverified means saying unverified" — we can curate on it only if the verification actually happens.
2. **It is cheap and non-blocking.** One fetch of `https://web.mit.edu/fnl/vol/171/fnl171.pdf` (or the `.htm`) from any session with working egress, confirming (i) the Bufferd byline and Pool A disclaimer sentence, and (ii) the seven Table II line items for the 2004/2003/2001 columns digit-for-digit. My timeout was an environment failure, not a 404 — the buildlog records the PDF as text-extractable and live. If the live URL ever dies, an Internet Archive capture of the same URL satisfies the gate (same document, independent host).

**Gate wording (binding):** MIT FY2001/FY2003/FY2004 allocation rows do not merge until one session other than the original worker has successfully fetched the FNL article (live or archived) and confirmed byline, disclaimer, and the three in-window Table II columns digit-for-digit. If the document cannot be retrieved by any means, the three years stay gapped — the ruling's yes is conditional on the source surviving independent retrieval. This does not delay the rest of 1.6 integration; it gates only these 24-ish rows, which can land in a follow-up commit.

### (b) Per-year Pool A coverage FY2001/FY2003 — the era-bracketing clause GOVERNS; the requester's per-year kill condition is self-defeating as written.

The requester's kill condition — "if the pool share can't be established per-year from primary documents, the year stays gapped" — applied honestly, kills the requester's own MIT outcome: no per-year share exists for FY2001 or FY2003, and per the buildlog the FY2000–FY2005 Reports of the Treasurer are **unrecoverable** (404 live, never captured by the Archive), so per-year evidence for those years is not pending — it is likely permanently unobtainable. A rule that demands unobtainable evidence while the same dataset ships those years' *returns* on the same Pool A basis is incoherent, not conservative.

The era-bracketing clause survives contact because the share figure is **admissibility evidence, not data** — the same evidence/data firewall as "audited figures are dating evidence, never data" (Harvard dating decision). Evidence about a structural fact (was Pool A substantially all of the endowment?) can legitimately be cumulative and era-level where the fact is structural and slow-moving, provided the README states exactly what was measured and when, and claims nothing per-year that wasn't measured. For FY2001–FY2004 the cumulative case is: (i) the Treasurer's own contemporaneous (Sept 2004) statement presenting Pool A as the endowment proxy, covering exactly these tables; (ii) 98.4% endowment-in-pool measured at FY2006; (iii) non-endowment content of the pool ~3.7% (FY2006) and still only ~7.5% (FY2011); (iv) MIT's 2008 congressional response titling the same era's Pool A return series "Investment Return to the Endowment" — the school's own universe attestation for these very years; (v) the −0.25pp-per-decade compounding residual, affirmative evidence of non-divergence. Three independent school-published attestations bracketing the era, no counter-evidence. That clears an evidence bar; a per-year-measurement bar is the wrong bar.

**However, the kill condition is retained in weakened, correct form** (see B2 below): if no primary evidence at all — neither per-year nor bracketing-with-attestation — establishes coverage, the year stays gapped. That is what actually decides Stanford's early years, where the requester's per-year condition does real work: applied to Stanford FY2000–FY2005 it yields "gap until verified," which collapses into my Phase 1 outcome (gap stands, upgrade path documented). "Possibly curatable pending verification" and "gapped with a documented upgrade path" are the same database state; the log should use the latter phrasing because a gap is the default, not a pending approval.

## 2. Reconciliation with Phase 1 — explicit phase-delta

**Ruling unchanged in option and in both instance outcomes.** Option B; MIT FY2001/03/04 curate (now with the fetch gate); Stanford stays gapped, all years, upgrade path documented; FY2004 store-actual. The requester's Phase 2 material moved me on exactly one point and I moved myself on one:

- **Changed (by self, prompted by the Phase 2 framing of the verification chain):** added the independent-fetch gate on the FNL source. My Phase 1 noted the failed fetch as "inconclusive, not adverse" but did not convert it into a gate. Phase 2's observation that the QC never covered the FNL either — leaving a single-session chain — makes the gate mandatory under K1. This is the phase-delta.
- **Not changed — threshold metric.** The requester's ≥90% endowment-share-OF-pool measures the wrong ratio. For a unitized pool, non-endowment money *inside* the pool distorts nothing: every participant holds identical units, so the pool's composition IS the endowment's composition for the fraction of the endowment invested through it. What makes pool percentages point the wrong way is endowment money *outside* the pool — Stanford's 12–15% direct real estate outside the Merged Pool is the documented mechanism of its 2.9pp return divergence, and it would survive the requester's test unexamined (a pool could be 100% endowment while holding half the endowment). The test is **≥95% of the endowment's investments held through the pool** (endowment-in-pool). MIT passes it at 98.4%. The requester's share-of-pool numbers (~96–98% MIT, ~97% Stanford FY2000, 73% modern Stanford) happen to sort the live instances the same way, which is why the error is worth correcting *now*, while it is outcome-invisible — the first pool that is endowment-pure but endowment-partial would flip a future outcome.
- **Not changed — Stanford early years.** "Possibly curatable" overstates it twice over: FY2000's ~97% is the wrong ratio (pool composition, not endowment coverage — what fraction of the endowment sat outside the pool in FY2000 is undocumented), and B1 as I frame it is not yet shown to be satisfiable — the pre-2016 "Endowment" headlines are contradicted by each document's own stated Merged Pool universe, and a headline over a contradicting document is not school-as-proxy attestation. Gap stands; the congressional responses are the named upgrade path.
- **Not changed — FY2004 disposition.** Requester and senior agree independently (blind convergence): store the actual, preserve the target in docs, no rail change mid-pilot.

## 3. FINAL RULING

**Option B.** Percentages a school published for its investment pool may be curated under the school's name **iff all four hold**:

- **B1 — School-as-proxy attestation.** The school itself, in a primary document, presented the pool's figures as describing its endowment. The measurement universe is what the document as a whole states, **never the table's headline label** — a label test rewards mislabeling (it would admit Stanford's mislabeled ~73% pool and exclude MIT's honestly-labeled 98.4% one).
- **B2 — Documented endowment-in-pool coverage ≥ 95%.** Primary-source evidence that the pool held ≥95% of the endowment's investments, at or bracketing the curated years. Era-bracketing evidence plus contemporaneous school attestation suffices **only when** per-year evidence is documented as unobtainable and the README states exactly which years were measured versus attested (K1). No evidence of either kind → the year stays gapped.
- **B3 — No affirmative divergence evidence.** Documented material divergence between pool and endowment figures in the relevant era fails the year regardless of B1/B2. Divergence evidence trumps the threshold.
- **B4 — Universe disclosed at every layer.** Source notes quote the school's own universe statement verbatim (including its hedges); the school's README section documents universe, evidence, and evidence dates; a visible caveat at the point of display (not caption-only); and where pool-universe years sit in one series with endowment-stated years, the basis break is annotated on the chart itself (Harvard mixed-basis treatment).

**Instance 1 — MIT FY2001/FY2003/FY2004: CURATE**, from FNL Table II (2004 column as `basis:"actual"` for FY2004), **gated on one successful independent fetch of the FNL article** (byline + Pool A disclaimer + three in-window columns digit-for-digit; live URL or Internet Archive capture of it; a different session than the original worker). If the document cannot be retrieved by any means, the three years stay gapped.

**Instance 2 — Stanford: GAP STANDS, all years, allocations and returns.** Modern era fails B2 (~85–88% coverage) and B3 (FY2024 ~2.9pp confirmed divergence; outside slice systematically concentrated in direct real estate). FY2000–FY2012 fails B1 (headline contradicted by the documents' own Merged Pool universe) and B2 (no endowment-in-pool evidence; FY2000's ~97% is pool composition — the wrong ratio). **Upgrade path, recorded in the README:** Stanford answered both congressional inquiries; if those or other primary documents establish ≥95% endowment-in-pool coverage with a non-concentrated residual for named years — or contain endowment-specific tables outright — those years become curatable under this same rule without relitigation.

**FY2004 collision: store the actual** (Table II 2004 column); Table III's target is preserved, transcribed, in the README/build log, not the database. No schema change — the validator's one-basis-per-school-year rule makes a basis-aware unique key moot for storage anyway, rails are stop-and-report mid-pilot, and the Princeton unit's actuals-over-targets call is the standing treatment. ## 4. Corrections to the requester's reasoning, for the record

1. **Wrong ratio in the threshold.** Endowment-share-of-pool is inert for a unitized pool (non-endowment co-investors hold identical units and distort nothing); the distorting quantity is endowment held *outside* the pool. The binding test is endowment-in-pool coverage ≥95%. The requester's numbers sort today's instances identically, which is precisely why the error must be corrected now, while it is outcome-invisible: an endowment-pure but endowment-partial pool would pass the requester's test and produce wrong-way numbers. Stanford is the proof — its divergence is driven by the 12–15% of the endowment outside the pool, not by the hospitals inside it.
2. **The per-year kill condition, applied as written, kills the requester's own MIT outcome** — per-year shares for FY2001/FY2003 are likely permanently unobtainable (source documents unrecoverable per the buildlog). The correct form is: *some* primary evidence (per-year, or era-bracketing plus contemporaneous school attestation) or the year gaps. Retained as B2.
3. **"Stanford early years possibly curatable" overstates the state.** FY2000's ~97% is the wrong ratio, and B1 is unsatisfied (headline contradicted by the document's own stated universe). The accurate record is: gapped, with a named, testable upgrade path. A gap is the default state, not a pending approval.
4. **Minor, for A4 hygiene:** the requester's condition (iii) puts the universe in `sourceLabel`; `sourceLabel` is defined as the school's own line-item wording, so the universe belongs in the source notes / README / display layers (B4), keeping `sourceLabel` a faithful transcription. Same disclosure, right container.
